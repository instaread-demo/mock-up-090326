Weekend spotlight: Comic con and concerts

The Catalyst’s every-Thursday guide to Tampa Bay entertainment and the arts.

Published

11 hours ago

on

September 2, 2026

By

Bill DeYoung

There’ll be photo ops aplenty at the 15th annual Tampa Bay Comic Con, Friday through Sunday at the Tampa Bay Convention Center (333 S. Franklin St., Tampa).

It’s a celebration of all things comics, including films, TV, graphic novels, online streaming, role-playing games and cosplay. There’s serious business in the form of writing workshops, makeup and artist tutorials and a lot of “how-to” events. Along with a massive vendors’ market.

“Celebrity Row” is a major attraction at these things. Recognizable names and faces have been rounded up and will sign autographs, and take photos with attendees, for a fee.

This weekend’s lineup includes iconic actress and singer Ann-Margret (although her connection to comics and/or superhero movies isn’t exactly clear), Ethan Peck (Star Trek: Strange New Worlds), grown-up ‘80s kid actors Soleil Moon Frye and Anthony Michael Hall, original Ghostbuster Ernie Hudson, wrestler/actor John Cena, Kane Hodder (Friday the 13th), wrestling legend Ric Flair, Rosalie Hale (The Twilight Saga), Austin St. John (Power Rangers), Ian Somerhalder (Lost), Holly Marie Combs (Charmed) and a whole lot more.

See the entire guest list, and do a little price comparison, here.

And here’s the main Tampa Bay Comic Con website.

 

Concert calendar

That Canadian group of professional musicians known as Classic Albums Live is back at Clearwater’s Capitol Theatre tonight at 8, performing Led Zeppelin’s self-titled debut album in its entirety. Find tickets at this link.

Friday at the BayCare Sound: It’s the annual 97X Next Big Thing concert, with Yellowcard, Waterparks, Dylan Cotrone, Saints of Saturn and 408. The show starts at 6 p.m., and tickets are here.

Chance the Rapper has a show Saturday at the Ritz Theatre in Ybor City. Chicago’s Chancelor Bennett was the first independent artist to win a Grammy with the mixtape Coloring Book; Saturday’s show is part of that album’s 10th anniversary tour. Showtime (with La Reezy) is 7 p.m., and tickets are at this link.

 

Geckos galore

Saturday brings the annual Gecko Fest to Gulfport’s Beach Boulevard, from 10 a.m. until 8 p.m. It’s an all-day vendor and food truck fest, with live music on two stages and street performers scattered from here to there.

The Gecko Parade steps off at 6 p.m. Morte details are in our story from earlier this week.

 

On theater stage

It’s your last weekend to catch the Jobsite Theater production of Penelope: An Epic New Musical in the Straz Center Shimberg Playhouse. Showtimes and tickets can be found here.
