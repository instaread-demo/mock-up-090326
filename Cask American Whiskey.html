Shortbarrel Launches Cigar Batch #4 Multi-Cask American Whiskey

What happens when you finish American whiskey in more than 50 separate casks, including Sherry for the first time? Shortbarrel's Cigar Batch #4 has the answer.

Atlanta-based Shortbarrel has released Cigar Batch #4, the fourth edition of its annual limited-release American Whiskey. The whiskey arrives in select retail markets from September 1, followed by a direct-to-consumer launch on Shortbarrel’s platform on Friday, September 4.

Cigar Batch #4 carries a suggested retail price of $89.99. It is bottled at 53.5% ABV/107 proof and is non-chill filtered.

The whiskey is built by blending separately finished components rather than relying on a single finishing cask type. This allows different mash bills, maturation profiles and finishing casks to each contribute to the final blend.

This year’s release incorporates whiskey finished in more than 50 separate Armagnac, Cognac, rum, Tawny Port and Sherry casks. Sherry casks appear in the Cigar Batch series for the first time. The finishing casks contribute layers of fruit, spice, oak and richness while allowing the underlying bourbon to remain at the center.

Cigar Batch #4 is built from mature bourbons and whiskeys with four different mash bills. These were distilled in Kentucky and Indiana before being selected and blended.

Each whiskey component underwent extended secondary maturation in its respective finishing cask before the final blend was assembled. While the whiskey begins its journey in Kentucky and Indiana, it takes its final shape in Georgia.

“Every barrel has a purpose,” said Clinton Dugan, co-founder and master blender at Shortbarrel. “Our responsibility is understanding what each component contributes, giving the whiskey the time it needs to develop and then finding the blend where those individual characteristics work together.”

Dugan added: “Cigar Batch has always been about building layers rather than allowing any single finish to dominate.”

According to the official notes from the brand, the nose offers dark cherry preserves, fig compote, candied orange peel, aged leather, pipe tobacco and cocoa nibs.

The palate carries Luxardo cherry, raisin, burnt sugar and milk chocolate, layered with cinnamon, clove, cigar wrapper and mature oak.

The finish is described as long and evolving, with dried fruit, cocoa powder, supple leather, tobacco leaf and persistent rye spice.

Shortbarrel recommends pairing the whiskey with the Arturo Fuente Don Carlos Robusto, Padrón 1964 Anniversary Maduro Exclusivo and Liga Privada No. 9 Toro cigars.

Shortbarrel’s retail distribution includes Georgia, Kentucky, Florida, Texas and Missouri. Products are also available through ecommerce.
