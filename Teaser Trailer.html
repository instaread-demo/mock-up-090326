Here’s the first official movie trailer of Hershey, the upcoming biography drama family movie starring Finn Wittrock, Alexandra Daddario, Francesca Faridany, Dina Spybey-Waters, Michael Moreland Milligan, Daniel David Stewart, Joel Brady, Alan Ruck, Richard Kind, and David Costabile:

HERSHEY

Based on the delicious true story.

From humble beginnings he built an empire of pure happiness.

Plot synopsis:
“The origin story of Milton and Kitty Hershey, who, when confronted by tragedy, determine to leave their amassed chocolate fortune in the hands of children.”

The film cast:
– Finn Wittrock as Milton S. Hershey
– Alexandra Daddario as Catherine “Kitty” Hershey
– Alan Ruck as Henry Hershey
– Richard Kind as Joseph Royer
– David Costabile as Tobias Thornhill
– Heléne Yorke as Margaret
– Michael Moreland Milligan as Lebbie
– Francesca Faridany as Veronica “Fanny” Hershey
– Daniel David Stewart as Murrie
– Dina Spybey as Aunt Mattie
