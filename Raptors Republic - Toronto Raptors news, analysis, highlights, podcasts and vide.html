Adam Silver just reconfirmed the Raptors as 2026-27 NBA championship contenders

Thanks, Adam!

September 2, 2026 Matthew Mountjoy

We now know the results of the NBA’s investigation, and boy, is it a steep punishment. However, the Raptors got a lot out of it. Let me explain why.

Before we get into it, let’s go over what has happened regarding the Kawhi Leonard situation.

Essentially, the Los Angeles Clippers and Kawhi Leonard were penalized for circumventing the salary cap on Wednesday. The team will forfeit five first-round picks in the 2029, 2031, 2032, and 2033 NBA drafts; owner Steve Ballmer will be suspended from all league and team activities for one year and fined $30 million; and two staffers will be suspended without pay for one year and six months, respectively.

As for Kawhi, he will have to pay $700,000 in restitution, and his former business representative, Dennis Robertson, has been banned from conducting business with the NBA and its affiliates for 5 years.

Now that there is context, let’s get into why NBA Commissioner Adam Silver put the Raptors on a quick path to success.

Kawhi is off to Toronto

Now, it is not a secret that Kawhi managed to get out of this investigation with a pretty light punishment.

With rumours swirling around that he could be suspended, have the contract voided, and the trade being dissolved, It is honestly best-case scenario that Leonard will only receive a fine, and the trade can be processed through business as usual.

Now, would it have been nice to keep Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and the draft capital if Kawhi’s contract were to be voided? Most likely, but as Mikail Nakhuda pointed out, Kawhi is a player who can completely change the ceiling for this team and put them on an immediate path to success if managed correctly — which is exactly what the Raptors did in the 2018-2019 season (jogging a few memories?).

Leonard is coming off a season in which he averaged 27.9 points, 6.4 rebounds, 3.6 assists, 1.9 steals, and 0.4 blocks across 64 games while shooting 50.5% from the field and 38.7% from three. Leonard was one of the bright spots on a Clippers squad that had a pretty abysmal start to the year before climbing their way to a 42-40 record.

Another point on top of this is his defensive prowess. Kawhi is heading to the team that finished the season with the fifth-best defensive rating at 113.0 and is widely regarded as one of the best defensive teams in the league, with much of the praise going to Scottie Barnes and Colin Murray-Boyles, with the former finishing fifth in Defensive Player of the Year voting. Adding his defensive ability to this roster will make this team even harder to play against.

Going back to the 2019 point earlier, Kawhi Leonard has championship pedigree tied to his experience bringing the team to their first championship seven years ago (time flies). Bringing his championship experience into a largely youthful core is a major plus for a Raptors squad that took the Cleveland Cavaliers to seven games in the 2026 NBA Playoffs.

I’m excited for what this team can do, and I’m looking forward to seeing The Klaw back in action wearing that iconic number 2 jersey.
