Kei Coffee House Celebrates Second Anniversary

The Westminster cafe is commemorating two years with new original characters, collectible merchandise, seasonal treats, and the campaign theme, “Sweet Moments Start Here.”

Westminster’s Kei Coffee House is celebrating its second anniversary! Designs for the cafe’s first original characters, Keira and her cat Donut, adorn a collection of limited-edition packaging, including cold beverage cups, pastry boxes, and takeout bags, plus merchandise such as a limited-edition Kei Coffee House mini tote bag. You can even win a free mini tote by completing the Passport Challenge–collect one stamp per drink purchase until you have six total.

Also part of the birthday festivities is a strawberries-and-cream series of lattes, matcha lattes, milks, and cookies. Plus, try the seasonal watermelon jasmine tea, watermelon matcha soda, and watermelon espresso tonic. For a savory option, a classic loco moco has also joined the menu. New sweets include the cookies & cream cookie and a decadent mango sticky rice with coconut sauce and a peanut sesame topping.

Customers can follow Keira and Donut through the seasons, as they will be back for future merch drops, collectibles, and limited-edition menu items.
